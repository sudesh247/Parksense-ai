# Overstay Penalties and Violation Policy

## Grace Period Rules
- A 15-minute complimentary grace period is allowed after payment for exiting the gate barrier.
- Exceeding the 15-minute post-payment grace period triggers an automated re-entry charge of 1 additional hour.

## Overstay Slabs and Fines
- Overstaying booked duration beyond 2 hours without online extension incurs a penalty of 1.5x the hourly rate for the extra duration.
- Continuous unauthorized occupancy exceeding 24 hours results in vehicle wheel clamping and a flat penalty fine of ₹1,000 in addition to total accumulated hourly charges.
- Unauthorized parking in designated EV charging or Disabled bays without authorization incurs an immediate penalty of ₹500.

## Vehicle Clamping and Towing
- Clamped vehicles will only be released upon full payment of accrued fines and presentation of valid vehicle ownership documentation at the administration booth.
- Abandoned vehicles uncollected after 7 days will be reported to municipal traffic police authorities.

## Dispute Resolution Process
- Payment disputes or billing queries can be submitted via the ParkSense dashboard support ticket system within 7 days of exit.
