# Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Policy

## EV Charging Infrastructure
- ParkSense features 12 dedicated EV charging bays equipped with AC Type-2 (7.4 kW / 22 kW) and DC Fast CCS-2 (50 kW) chargers.
- EV charging slots are available on Floor 1 (Slots F1-A02, F1-A05, F1-A08, F1-A12) and Floor 2 (Slots F2-B03, F2-B07, F2-B10, F2-B14).

## EV Tariff and Electricity Surcharge
- Parking in an EV slot incurs a standard ₹10 per hour slot surcharge in addition to the base vehicle parking rate.
- Electricity consumption for active charging is billed separately at ₹15 per kWh based on meter readings.

## Idle Occupancy Surcharge
- To ensure fair access, an Idle Fee of ₹50 per 30 minutes applies if an EV remains parked in a charging bay for more than 20 minutes after reaching 100% state of charge.
- Non-electric vehicles (ICE engines) parked in EV charging slots will be clamped immediately with a ₹500 fine.
