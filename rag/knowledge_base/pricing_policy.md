# Parking Fee Structure and Tariff Policy

## Hourly Pricing Rates
- Two-Wheelers (Bikes/Scooters): ₹20 per hour or fraction thereof across all floors.
- Standard Cars (Sedans/Hatchbacks): ₹40 per hour on Floor 1 & Floor 2 (Prime Zone); ₹35 per hour on Floor 3 & Floor 4.
- Heavy Utility Vehicles (SUVs/MUVs): ₹60 per hour across all floors.
- EV Charging Slots: Applicable base vehicle rate + ₹10 per hour EV surcharge.

## Billing Calculation Rules
- Minimum billing block is 1 hour. Any partial duration exceeding 15 minutes is rounded up to the full next hour.
- A maximum daily cap of ₹350 applies for continuous 24-hour parking for standard cars, and ₹150 for two-wheelers.

## Discount Programs and Passes
- Monthly Unlimited Executive Pass: ₹3,500 per month for reserved car slots, subject to availability.
- Night Parking Tariff (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM): Flat 20% discount on standard hourly rates for registered app users.
- Weekend Flat Rate: Standard hourly pricing applies; no additional weekend surcharges are levied.
