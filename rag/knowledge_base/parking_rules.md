# General Parking Facility Rules & Operational Guidelines

## Entry and Exit Regulations
- All vehicles entering ParkSense Facility must stop at the automated boom barrier for license plate scanning and digitised ticket issuance.
- The maximum speed limit within all indoor parking floors is 10 km/h. Hazard lights must be used when reversing into a parking bay.
- Drivers must park strictly within the painted lines of their assigned slot. Parking across multiple bays or in driveways is strictly prohibited.

## Safety and Security Policies
- Smoking, open flames, and storage of volatile hazardous materials within the parking building are strictly forbidden.
- ParkSense management is not responsible for lost items, theft, or natural damage to personal property left inside vehicles.
- CCTV surveillance is operational 24/7 across all floors, ramps, and stairwells for security compliance.

## Vehicle Eligibility and Standards
- Commercial heavy vehicles exceeding 3.5 tonnes gross weight are not permitted inside the facility.
- Motorized two-wheelers must be parked only in dedicated bike slots located on designated areas.
- Unauthorized commercial selling, washing, or mechanical repair of vehicles on facility premises is prohibited.
