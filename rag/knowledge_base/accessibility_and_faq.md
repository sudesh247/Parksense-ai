# Accessibility Guidelines & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Accessible (Disabled-Friendly) Parking
- ParkSense provides 6 widened accessible parking bays located immediately adjacent to main elevator lobbies and exit ramps on Floors 1, 2, and 3.
- Accessible bays feature extra 1.5m clearance aisles for wheelchair ramps.
- Display of a valid government-issued disability parking permit or blue badge on the vehicle dashboard is mandatory when using accessible bays.

## Booking Cancellations and Refund Policy
- Active bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 15 minutes before the scheduled arrival entry time.
- Cancellations requested within 15 minutes of scheduled entry incur a 50% cancellation fee.
- Once a vehicle has entered the gate barrier, the booking cannot be cancelled; standard hourly exit rates apply upon release.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if I lose my digital parking ticket?
Contact the parking administration office at the main entrance barrier. Verification of vehicle registration certificate (RC) and driver ID is required, followed by payment of the flat Lost Ticket Fee of ₹300 plus actual parked duration charges.

### Can I reserve a specific parking slot in advance?
Yes, users can pre-book available or designated reserved slots via the ParkSense web dashboard under the Slots section.

### How do I release my booked slot when leaving?
You can navigate to the Bookings section on the web dashboard and click the "Release Slot" button. The system will automatically calculate the final fee based on entry and exit time, free up the slot, and update slot status to available.
